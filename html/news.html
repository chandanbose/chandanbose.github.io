All News

  1. Nov 2026

    BioFSILab will present its latest research at the 79th APS Division of Fluid Dynamics Annual Meeting in Orlando, USA (22–24 November), including butterfly flight aerodynamics, passively morphing foils in gusty flows and data-driven modelling of vortex-induced vibrations.

  2. Aug 2026

    Our paper “Effect of transverse gust on free-falling plates” has been published in the Journal of Fluid Mechanics, in collaboration with the University of Edinburgh.

  3. Jul 2026

    Our work on GPU-optimised integration of immersed-boundary and overset-mesh methods for moving-boundary problems has been published in Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, with collaborators at IIT Kharagpur.

  4. Jun 2026

    New preprint with Georgia Tech: multi-objective Bayesian optimisation of rigid and flexible nozzles for energy-efficient pulsed-jet propulsion.

  5. May 2026

    Doudou Huang’s paper on the wake dynamics of permeable disks across the transition from steady to chaotic flow has been published in the Journal of Fluid Mechanics, and our collaborative preprint “Squid funnels time their elastic recoil to amplify pulsed jets” is now on arXiv.

  1. Dec 2025

    Dr. Chandan Bose, Miss. Hibah Saddal, and Mr. Arjun Shergill will present the group's research on fluid-structure interaction and GPU-accelerated computing in the CoSeC Annual Conference and CIUK2025 conference 2025 in Manchester, UK.

  2. Nov 2025

    Dr. Chandan Bose has been awarded the Computational Science Centre for Research Communities (CoSeC) Fellowship 2025, STFC, UK.

  3. Nov 2025

    Mr. Paras Singh and Miss. Doudou Huang represented BioFSILab at the American Physical Society Division of Fluid Mechanics Annual Meeting 2025.

  4. Nov 2025

    Miss. Hibah Saddal and Dr. Chandan Bose have been awarded the national image prize by EPCC ARCHER2 for their research on morphing wings.

  5. Nov 2025

    A total of nine simulation images and videos featuring the research carried out in BioFSILab have been featured in the EPCC ARCHER2 Image and Video Contest 2025.

  1. July 2024

    Dr. Chandan Bose has been selected as a Guest Associate Editor in the “Journal of Computational Sciences, Elsevier”.

  2. July 2024

  3. July 2024

Prof. Marco de Tullio, Prof. Oscar Flores, and Prof. Ramiro Godoy-Diana are also on the organizing team. The Colloquium will be held in Vienna (Austria) from 9 to 11 July 2025.
